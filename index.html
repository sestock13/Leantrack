How it works

On Friday and Saturday, compare completed days since Monday with your normal target. Only a fully logged period can unlock an earned-beer estimate. It's just a way to visualize naturally unused calories, not a reason to eat less or a separate alcohol allowance. Your drinks still count toward daily intake, and nothing carries over to another week.

Apple Health connection

V3

Apple Shortcuts sends the daily totals that Lose It writes to Health to your private Cloudflare Worker. LeanTrack fetches those numbers when you open it.

Your key is saved only in this browser's local storage, never in your GitHub repository. Use a separate WRITE_KEY in Shortcuts. Health data is stored in your Cloudflare account.

Setup guide

1. Create Cloudflare KV and a Worker. 2. Save private READ_KEY and WRITE_KEY secrets. 3. Add a daily Apple Shortcut. 4. Enter the Worker URL and READ_KEY here.

Open the included SETUP-GUIDE.md for detailed steps, including the precise Shortcut actions.
